# About Me

Hello! My name is David Amezcua Santiago, and I am currently a senior pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering at Iowa State University, with an expected graduation date of May 2027.

Before attending Iowa State University, I earned my Associate of Science in Engineering from Des Moines Area Community College.

My interests include Linux, software development, computer hardware, embedded systems, and artificial intelligence. I enjoy exploring how hardware and software interact and learning new technologies through hands-on projects.

Throughout my academic journey, I have gained experience working with programming languages such as C, C++, Python, and Java, along with hardware technologies including RISC-V, Verilog, VHDL, and FPGAs.

I have also participated in undergraduate research involving artificial intelligence and atomic force microscopy image analysis. Currently, I am working on a senior design project that explores how large language models and AI agents can assist with circuit card design.

Outside of coursework, I enjoy experimenting with Linux environments, customizing my development setup, and expanding my technical knowledge.

My goal is to continue developing my engineering skills and pursue a career where I can contribute to innovative hardware and software technologies.
